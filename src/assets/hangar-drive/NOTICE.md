# Hangar Drive logo: source and license

The sidebar, the session menu and the settings show Hangar Drive's logo where Hangar can hand a
project to it (ADR 0024). `hangar-drive.svg` is the app icon of Hangar Drive (`src-tauri/app-icon.svg`
in its repository), by the same author as Hangar and released under the MIT license. The file is
unchanged; the app reads its shapes and colors from it and draws them inline (DriveMark.tsx).
